In the morning, Dani's back was better, but still not good. And then there was a rain front coming, so we decided to prolong our stay in the hotel for one more night and to do some sightseeing.

There was a food and farmers' market which was insanely crowded. And then there was a nice classical concert. Also, the _Open Art_ festival is currently going on, with exhibits throughout the city.

There were rain showers throughout the day, some of them quite heavy — so we were happy to have made the right choice to spend an extra day in the city.
