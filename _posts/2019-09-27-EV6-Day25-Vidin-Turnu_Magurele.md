Today was my longest day on a bicycle, ever (192 km!). 😁 The tailwind was just amazing, and I wanted to make use of it (and I guess to make up for the half-lost day from yesterday).

I did some calculations and figured out that if I hurried, I could make it all the way to Constanța on the Black Sea (and thereby complete the eastern part of the EuroVelo 6) before heading back to Bucharest for the wedding.

So I raced through the villages. I like the countryside in Romania. Lots of horse carts and friendly people in the villages. However, unfortunately also lots of trash and roadkill at the side of the roads.
