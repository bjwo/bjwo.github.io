Today we are taking the ferry from Vaasa in Finland to Holmsund (Umeå) in Sweden. We spent a week with my family in Finland and in the Kvarken Archipelago.

Trying to plan for rainy days and cold nights in Sweden while also packing light is not so easy, and you are constantly worried that you forgot something vital. But now we're on the ship and feel ready and psyched for our coming bicycling adventure along the EuroVelo 7. 😊

On my bike trip last year I met another bike traveler on the way out from Berlin. She is also on a bike trip along the EV10 this year, and by chance she arrived in Vaasa today. It was fun to meet again, and she came to bid us farewell at the ferry.
