I left the hostel rather early — which proved futile, because on the way I found a shop which had cooking gas cartridges, but it only opened later. So I had to hang out for an hour in front of the shop and had some breakfast.

A little later, when leaving the suburbs of Belgrade, I had to choose between riding on the dam — where the road was in a very bad shape — or riding on the highway. I chose the latter and made it to the ferry across the Danube at Stara Palanka earlier than expected. Note: the [ferry crossing point is here](https://goo.gl/maps/fMPHJ3cfb2XXWts89), in contrast to what the maps make you believe.

The weather was also on my side — and fortunately it didn't rain all day, in contrast to what the weather report had predicted.

The ferry was rather interesting: it didn't have its own motor, and it docked to the land by shovelling dirt and gravel in between the ferry and the land.

I made it to a nice little camp site on the other side of the river. The standard price for a room here in the Serbian countryside always seems to be the equivalent of 10 € in RSD.
