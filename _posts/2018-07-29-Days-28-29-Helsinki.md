Dani — my girlfriend — was supposed to arrive around noon in Helsinki. Unfortunately, she was re-booked from a direct flight from Stuttgart to Helsinki to a flight via Munich. Even more unfortunately, there was a security incident in Munich that morning: a woman had entered the gated area of the airport without going through the security checks. This caused complete chaos in Munich, and it wasn't clear whether Dani could fly to Helsinki at all on that date.

Later it turned out that Dani could fly, but her bicycle did not come with her — and it was unclear where the bike was left (Stuttgart? Munich?). So lots of chaos and uncertainty, especially as we had already booked our train to travel to Vaasa to see my family.

In the end it all turned out well. :) I was able to do some sightseeing in Helsinki with Dani, and on the morning before we had to catch the train we were able to collect her bike from the airport.
