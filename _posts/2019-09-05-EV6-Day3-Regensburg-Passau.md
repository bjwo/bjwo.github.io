Today's weather turned out better than predicted. There was some drizzling rain, but plenty of wind from behind — yay!

I made it to Passau. I had the best Cordon Bleu of my life when I was in Passau two years before — so I saved up all my hunger for the evening, looking forward to getting that great Cordon Bleu. I don't think you can imagine my disappointment when I saw that the place was closed for vacation. 😔 FYI, the place is called _kreuzweis_.

Again I am staying at the camp site of the local canoe club. When I got there, there was some heavy rain — which hasn't stopped yet. Fortunately, there is a communal dining area with a roof. So I didn't have to put up my tent in the rain, but just found a spot in the corner of the common area.
