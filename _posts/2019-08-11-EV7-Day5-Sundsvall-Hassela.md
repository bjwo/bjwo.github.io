We did not get much sleep last night… Our vindskydd roof was leaking and the strong wind blew the rain right into our shelter. Thus our tent was wet in the morning.
Furthermore, a couple of teenagers who went for a midnight swim in the rain kept us awake.
So we were not very excited about cycling in the rain. Even more so since we knew that we were now about to leave the coast for good, and the next considerable village — Hassela — was 50 km and about 600 hm away.
However, it had stopped raining, and the forecast promised only a little more rain and even some sunshine for the afternoon.

As we reached Hassela, we were soaked, tired and hungry. Therefore we decided to stay in the local hostel (Vandrarhem). It's a very nice place, and as it happens we have it all to ourselves. We were able to do our laundry and dry our stuff in a special drying cabinet.
We did get to see the sun later. But given the violent rain showers throughout the afternoon, it was a wise decision to stay in…
