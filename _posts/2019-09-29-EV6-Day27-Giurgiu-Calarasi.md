I continued racing through the landscape and was making very good time until I decided to have lunch at a restaurant.
After a little while the owner started chatting with me, and we probably talked for close to three hours. It was great, and I learnt a lot about Romania again — however, when you try to make it to your accommodation in daylight, you always feel rushed. ☺️

Again, there was no cheap room to be found, so ultimately I booked a room for ~25 € at a hotel. And it turned out to be huge. I would have preferred something smaller, more simple and cheaper, though. 😉

Oh, and of course I didn't make it in daylight.
