I mainly used my time in Budapest to revisit old places, drink some beer, meet some people, eat good food and relax. It was great. Plenty of tourists here, though — I guess as a local I'd be pretty annoyed.

The cycling infrastructure is poor, though. 😔 It's hard to get around by bike: even where there are painted bike lanes, they often stop abruptly, leaving you in the middle of car and bus traffic.
