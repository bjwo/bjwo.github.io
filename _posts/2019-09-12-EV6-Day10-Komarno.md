I took this day off from cycling, as I still felt sick and was coughing quite a bit.

Included in the fee for my camp site was entry to the thermal bath next door. So I tried to kill all the bacteria by boiling myself in hot water for a day. 😉

Oh, and in the morning I met Max from NZ, who was staying at the same camp site. He has an inflatable raft which packs down into a backpack, so he's touring the world with his little raft. Last month he'd been to Iraq — now he's travelling down the Danube. We're planning to catch up downstream in Budapest.
