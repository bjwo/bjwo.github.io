Today was the first day cycling with my girlfriend. And oh boy, we were off to a rough ride: there was a strong, storm-like headwind all day, with gusts of up to 80 km/h. We still managed to do close to 100 km today — very proud. 😊💪🌬️ We found a quiet and cosy spot for our tent on a beach on a little island near Närpes. 🏕️🚴‍♀️🚴💨<br><br>
Oh, and we cycled around a gigantic meteorite crater near Vaasa today — that was very impressive.
